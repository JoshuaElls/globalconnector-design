Situation 01

Bring Us In at the Start of a New Program

Too many OEMs treat the cable and connector as an afterthought — something to source once the device is designed. By then, connector choices, materials, and routing are locked in, and every compromise shows up later as cost, schedule slips, or reliability problems. Engage Gii early and our engineers help define the interconnect while it’s still easy to get right.

Sounds like you if…

  • Your team is strong on the device, less so on cable and connector design
  • You want manufacturability designed in, not fixed later
  • You need a partner who’ll still be there at volume production

How Gii helps

  • Requirements definition for the cable and connector
  • Custom connector design or build-to-print — whichever fits
  • DFM reviews before design freeze
  • US-based engineering and rapid prototyping
  • A single team from prototype through volume production

Situation 02

Design a Connector When Nothing Off-the-Shelf Fits

Energy-based devices push interconnects hard — high voltage, tight profiles, sterilization, and single-use economics. When a branded catalog connector can’t meet your requirements or your cost target, Gii’s engineers design one that does.

Sounds like you if…

  • Catalog connectors force compromises on size, voltage, or mating
  • You’re building an RF, PFA, cryo, or electrosurgical system
  • You need single-use economics without sacrificing performance
  • You want a proprietary interface for your system

How Gii helps

  • Push-pull, locking, non-locking, and breakaway designs
  • 1–30+ contacts, high-voltage rated per IEC 60601
  • Biocompatible materials and custom overmolded housings
  • Single-use and reusable options
  • Fully proprietary architectures
